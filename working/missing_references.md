# Missing References — Action List

Papers cited in the argument but not yet in `study/references.bib`. Find, download, and add BibTeX.

## Must Have (core argument)

- [ ] **Suddaby, R. (2010).** Construct clarity in theories of management and organization. *Academy of Management Review*, 35(3), 346-357.
  - **Why:** Defines the construct clarity criteria used to build the Ecosystem Stewardship construct (what/not/boundary/relation). Core method paper.

- [ ] **Teece, D.J. (2007).** Explicating dynamic capabilities: the nature and microfoundations of (sustainable) enterprise performance. *Strategic Management Journal*, 28(13), 1319-1350.
  - **Why:** The specific DC paper that introduces sensing/seizing/transforming. Have 1997 and 2018 but not the 2007 microfoundations paper. Root of Mechanism 2 (Portfolio Transfer).

- [ ] **Williamson, O.E. (1985).** *The Economic Institutions of Capitalism.* Free Press, New York.
  - **Why:** The foundational TCE book, not just the articles. Core of the opportunism assumption being challenged.

- [ ] **Tushman, M.L. (1977).** Special boundary roles in the innovation process. *Administrative Science Quarterly*, 22(4), 587-605.
  - **Why:** Root of Mechanism 3 (Boundary Management). The original boundary-spanning paper. Have Tushman 1996 (ambidexterity) but not the 1977 boundary roles paper.

## Should Have (structural context)

- [ ] **Porter, M.E. (1996).** What is strategy? *Harvard Business Review*, 74(6), 61-78.
  - **Why:** The "stuck in the middle" argument. Frames the strategic vacuum for non-platform firms. Have Porter 1985 (Competitive Advantage) but not the 1996 HBR.

- [ ] **Simon, H. (2009).** *Hidden Champions of the Twenty-First Century.* Springer.
  - **Why:** Mid-size niche world leaders through focus + deep client knowledge + continuous innovation. The empirical precedent for SBP's position. Lab271 as Hidden Champions engine.

- [ ] **Jacobides, M.G., Knudsen, T. & Augier, M. (2006).** Benefiting from innovation: value creation, value appropriation and the role of industry architectures. *Research Policy*, 35(8), 1200-1221.
  - **Why:** Industry architecture — how value migrates across layers. The theoretical anchor for the mid-size squeeze argument. Note: different from Jacobides 2006 "Designing the Boundaries" already in bib.
